Transparent pricing — proposed, not yet live.

The numbers below are launch targets we intend to validate against real measured costs. No billing is active, no payment is collected, and no one is charged today.

Proposed launch pricing — not yet live, no billing active

TierInput / 1M tokensOutput / 1M tokensBest for
Economy$0.15$0.60Background jobs, prototypes, price-sensitive workloads
Standard$0.30$1.20General chat and agent workloads

Prices are targets. They will only become real prices after benchmarking measures actual tokens-per-second, electricity and hardware costs on provider machines, and after the pricing engine accounts for failed requests and infrastructure expenses. If measured costs do not support a tier, the tier changes — the table does not.

Free testing during beta

During the beta, testing access is free within available resource limits. That means: use the playground and the demo API while capacity lasts, with fair-use rate limits (demo: 60 requests/minute per key). Free beta access is not a promise of unlimited free inference forever.

Provider payouts — a proposal, pending validation

The proposed split is 80% to the provider / 20% to the platform on paid completed work. This is a proposal, not a contract: it depends on measured operating costs, payment-processing fees, and whether the economics actually work at the prices above. No earnings are guaranteed. Providers are paid only for successfully completed paid workloads, and only once paid billing exists and provider compensation can actually be funded.

Paid billing will not be activated until: the service is reliable, pricing has been validated against real benchmarks, legal requirements are understood, payment processing is available, and provider compensation can actually be funded.
