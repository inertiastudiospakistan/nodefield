Privacy policy.

Pre-launch draft. Last updated: November 2026. This policy will be revised before any production launch; the version in force at launch will be published here.

What we collect

Through the waitlist forms we collect only what you enter: for providers — name or username, email, country, GPU manufacturer and model, GPU VRAM, system RAM, operating system, internet connection type, available hours, and whether you are interested in beta testing; for developers — email, intended application, expected monthly token usage, preferred models, and desired API features. The demo API also records API usage (requests, tokens, errors) for rate limiting and billing validation. We do not collect passwords for external services, and we do not ask for payment details.

How we use it

To contact you about the waitlist and beta, to understand what hardware and demand the network will have, to operate and secure the demo API, and to improve the product. API keys are stored hashed server-side; only key prefixes are ever listed.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use the GPU agent to access personal files on provider machines — it reads hardware information and runs the inference runtime only.

Storage and retention

Waitlist data is stored in the project database (demo: local storage in your browser if the backend is unreachable — see the registration page). We will delete your waitlist entry on request. Contact details for deletion requests will be published here before launch.

Cookies and analytics

This static site sets no tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics. The demo backend may log requests for operational purposes only.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be noted here with a new date before they take effect.
