How models are chosen

Small (1B–4B) first for broad compatibility, then medium (7B–8B), then larger (12B–14B) for machines with 12 GB+ VRAM. A model is only listed as supported once it has actually run and been benchmarked on representative provider hardware — never on a spec sheet alone.
