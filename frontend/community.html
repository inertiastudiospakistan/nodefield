Guidelines

Be honest about what works. No spam, no fake accounts, no misleading earnings claims, no harassment. Technical discussion over hype.

Community guidelines

  • Talk about real hardware, real benchmarks and real failures. A failed benchmark is useful; a made-up one gets you removed.
  • No spam, no unsolicited promotion, no multiple accounts.
  • Do not promise earnings to other providers — nobody can, yet.
  • Help newcomers install and benchmark; report bugs with steps to reproduce.
  • Respect that provider machines are people’s personal computers: consent and control come first.
