Earn from your unused GPU.

If your graphics card spends most of its time idle, Nodefield is building a way to put it to work — on your terms, with your control, and with no promise of earnings we cannot back up.

How providing works

  1. Register. Tell us your GPU, VRAM, operating system and availability on the waitlist. No payment details, no passwords for other services.
  2. Install the agent (beta). A lightweight desktop app detects your hardware, benchmarks it locally, and reports capacity to the coordinator.
  3. Set your limits. Choose when you are available and how much VRAM and power the agent may use. Pause any time.
  4. Complete work, get paid — once billing exists. Providers are paid only when their hardware successfully completes paid workloads. The proposed split is 80% provider / 20% platform, pending validation. No billing is active yet, so no earnings exist yet.
No earnings are guaranteed. Income depends on real developer demand, your hardware, your uptime, and prices that are still being validated. A machine that is merely connected earns nothing.

GPU compatibility

Compatibility is about VRAM, drivers and a supported inference runtime — not brand loyalty. The table below is our current guidance; the agent verifies your actual hardware before any work is assigned.

HardwareStatusNotes
NVIDIA RTX 3060 (12 GB), 4060 Ti (16 GB), 4070 and aboveCompatibleEnough VRAM for 7B–8B quantised models; CUDA support.
NVIDIA RTX 3060 (8 GB), RTX 4060, GTX 16-series with 6 GB+CompatibleSuits 1B–4B models; 8B models only where VRAM allows.
AMD RX 6000 / 7000 series (8 GB+)ExperimentalSupported where the runtime supports it; performance varies by driver and OS.
GPUs with under 4 GB VRAM, integrated graphics onlyUnsupportedToo little VRAM for the launch models.
macOS (Apple Silicon)ExperimentalPossible for small models via supported runtimes; Windows and Linux are the first targets.

VRAM figures are for Q4-quantised launch models; see models.

Security — what the agent will never do

Never arbitrary code

The agent never executes arbitrary code supplied by marketplace customers. Workloads are sandboxed, validated inference jobs only — a model, a prompt, and strict resource limits.

Never hidden activity

Everything the agent does is visible in its dashboard: utilisation, VRAM, jobs completed, uptime. It never secretly uses your GPU.

Never your files

The agent does not access personal files. It reads hardware information and runs the inference runtime — nothing else.

Always your switch

Pause participation any time, set resource limits, or uninstall completely. Signed updates, encrypted coordinator traffic, and an emergency shutdown are part of the design.
