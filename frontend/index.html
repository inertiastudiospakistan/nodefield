What Nodefield is

Idle hardware, meet real demand

Millions of capable GPUs sit idle in gaming PCs, workstations and small servers while developers pay premium prices for AI inference. Nodefield is building an open marketplace that connects the two: GPU owners contribute capacity they are not using, developers call open-weight models through a simple OpenAI-compatible API, and the scheduler routes each request to a single suitable machine.

For GPU owners

Earn from your unused GPU — when your hardware actually completes paid work. You stay in control: pause any time, set resource limits, uninstall any time. No earnings are guaranteed.

How it works

Three steps, no hype

  1. Providers install the Nodefield agent. It detects your GPU and VRAM, runs a local benchmark, and reports available capacity to the coordinator. It never runs arbitrary customer code and never touches your personal files.
  2. Developers send a normal API request. One endpoint, OpenAI-compatible. The scheduler assigns the whole request to one suitable machine — we do not split a single model across geographically distributed consumer GPUs.
  3. Work completes, usage is reported. Tokens in and out are counted, results return to the developer, and — once paid billing exists — the provider is paid for completed work under the published split.
