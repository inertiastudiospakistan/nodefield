## Server structure

### INFORMATION

- **#announcements** — launch news, releases, and major updates only. Admins/mods post; everyone reads.
- **#roadmap** — public roadmap (link to `ROADMAP.md`); what phase we're in and what's next.
- **#rules** — community guidelines (below). New members must be able to find them in one click.

### COMMUNITY

- **#general** — open discussion about AI inference, GPUs, and the project.
- **#introductions** — new members: who you are, what GPU you have (if any), what you want to build.
- **#hardware-discussion** — GPUs, rigs, drivers, VRAM, cooling, electricity costs. Real photos and real specs welcome.

### CONTRIBUTORS

- **#beta-access** — beta signup, eligibility, and rollout updates.
- **#installation-help** — agent install and troubleshooting; helpers answer with exact steps, not guesses.
- **#gpu-benchmarks** — members post benchmark results (hardware, model, quant, method — same standard as `BENCHMARKS.md`).
- **#provider-feedback** — what contributors need from the platform: payouts, controls, reporting.

### DEVELOPERS

- **#api-help** — integration questions, SDKs, error messages (paste the full error, not a paraphrase).
- **#model-discussion** — which models to support, quality, licences.
- **#integration-help** — swapping base URLs, frameworks, agents, edge cases.

### DEVELOPMENT

- **#progress-updates** — weekly dev updates from the team (honest: shipped / didn't ship / blocked).
- **#bug-reports** — bug template: what happened, what you expected, steps, environment.
- **#feature-requests** — one request per post; upvotes signal demand, they don't guarantee a build.

## Community guidelines

1. **Be kind.** Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, no slurs, no personal attacks.
2. **No spam.** No unsolicited DMs selling things, no repeated posting, no referral-link farming.
3. **No fake earnings claims.** Don't promise anyone income from Nodefield, and don't post "earnings" that aren't real completed payouts. There are no payouts yet.
4. **No crypto/token talk.** Nodefield has no token and never will (see ADR-006). Don't promote tokens, coins, or mining schemes here.
5. **No fake numbers.** Benchmarks need hardware, model, quant, and method — or they're not benchmarks.
6. **Help, don't gatekeep.** Beginners asking basic questions are welcome; answer plainly or point to the docs.
7. **Keep it legal.** No pirated models, no licence violations, no instructions for wrongdoing.
8. **Mods' word is final on conduct.** Appeals by DM to a mod; don't relitigate in public channels.
